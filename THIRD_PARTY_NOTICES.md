# Third-party sources

The voice firmware and the old Rust ASR client were removed on 2026-09-22. On 2026-09-26 the
firmware returned as a display-only client with the unchanged display components below; the
voice-only libfvad and the ASR client stay retired, their license texts under
`docs/history/retired-licenses/` using the former source paths. The QR library of the VPN invite
page, removed on 2026-10-08, is recorded there the same way. Local ASR/VAD/TTS packages
described below are no longer required by the web service. The local model evaluations
(FunctionGemma, LFM2.5, Qwen3.5, Smart Turn) and their demo services were removed on 2026-10-10;
their model manifests and the Smart Turn license moved to
`docs/history/retired-licenses/server/models/`.

Firmware uses ESP-IDF **v5.5.2**, commit `30aaf64524299d3bde422ca9a2848090d1bc5d0f`, from Espressif. SDK dependencies retain their upstream licenses and live outside this source bundle.

The display components are taken from Waveshare's [ESP32-S3-RLCD-4.2 repository](https://github.com/waveshareteam/ESP32-S3-RLCD-4.2/tree/eb1f63427d735a22b9c30e22fa63ebddae1834d3/02_Example/ESP-IDF/11_U8G2_Test/components), revision `eb1f63427d735a22b9c30e22fa63ebddae1834d3`:

- `firmware/components/u8g2_st7305`: driver and component definition; Apache-2.0, copyright 2026 Waveshare. Local modification on 2026-09-10: display inversion disabled (0x20 instead of 0x21) for dark text on a light background. The upstream license is included alongside the driver.
- `firmware/components/u8g2`: unchanged bundled U8g2 component; BSD-2-Clause for the library, separate font terms documented in the included `LICENSE` and source headers. Its upstream CMake excludes unrelated display drivers.

OpenUsage at revision `70dea9a8fa21ed205aa9ad625b416a1e7792d5a1` was consulted for HTTP routes and response formats. Its Swift implementation was not copied. The Python probe and device application are separate implementations.

Official Codex and Claude Code CLIs run on enrolled worker machines under their owner's
native authentication. They are not included in the firmware or vendored into this source bundle.

## Weather data: Open-Meteo

The `weather` plugin reads forecasts, the ECMWF EC46 weekly outlook and place names from the
public Open-Meteo APIs (https://open-meteo.com/) at request time; nothing of Open-Meteo is
vendored. Weather data by Open-Meteo.com, licensed under CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); Open-Meteo combines open data of national weather
services (ECMWF, DWD, NOAA, Météo-France, JMA, MET Norway and others) and the GeoNames gazetteer
(CC BY 4.0). Answers built from a forecast link Open-Meteo as their source. The free API is for
non-commercial use (600 calls a minute, 10 000 a day); the server identifies itself as
`DeviceAssistant/1.0 (+https://github.com/chromvoid/bro)` and caches one forecast per ~1 km cell.

## Place names: Nominatim and OpenStreetMap

The server names a person's place (a district for the turn, an address for a stay) by reverse
geocoding through the public Nominatim service of the OpenStreetMap Foundation
(https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org/), within its usage policy: at most one request a second,
an identifying User-Agent and cached results; coordinates leave rounded to three decimals.
Nothing of Nominatim is vendored. Data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open
Database License (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright); the app shows this attribution next
to the place.

## WHOOP sleep stages: PhysioNet sleep-accel

The light/deep/REM weights in `server/plugins/personal/whoop_stages_model.py` were fitted by
`tools/whoop_sleep_stages/train.py` on «Motion and heart rate from a wrist-worn wearable and
labeled sleep from polysomnography» 1.0.0 by Olivia Walch (PhysioNet, 2019,
https://physionet.org/content/sleep-accel/1.0.0/, DOI 10.13026/hmhs-py35, RRID:SCR_007345),
licensed under the Open Data Commons Attribution License v1.0
(https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/). Original publication: Walch O, Huang Y, Forger D,
Goldstein C. Sleep stage prediction with raw acceleration and photoplethysmography heart rate data
derived from a consumer wearable device. Sleep, https://doi.org/10.1093/sleep/zsz180. The data are
not in this repository; only the fitted coefficients are.
